✈️ Trip Planner & Smart Scheduler - JC studio
A specialized tool designed to transform flight details into a structured business itinerary with zero manual re-entry.

🌟 Core Functionalities
1. Integrated Flight Plan Generation
Multi-Leg Input: Input detailed flight information including airports, flight numbers, and local take-off/landing times.

One-Click Consolidation: Click "Generate Flight" to instantly produce a clean, summarized Flight Overview Table for your entire journey.

2. Smart Schedule Migration (New!)
Data Inheritance: When you click "Generate Schedule", the system automatically scans your flight plan and populates those details into a time-based daily itinerary.

Auto-Row Insertion: If a flight occurs at a specific time (e.g., 15:45), the schedule will automatically insert a new row at that exact time slot and mark the flight details (Flight #, Route) for you.

Seamless Transition: Eliminates the need to copy-paste flight times from your tickets into your daily schedule.

3. Professional Itinerary Management
Visual Customization: Color-code your tasks (Meetings, Meals, Flights) using the built-in color palette.

Cell Merging: Select multiple slots to block out long-duration activities.

Dual Excel Export: Export both your "Flight Overview" and your "Daily Schedule" into formatted Excel files separately.

🚀 Workflow
Define Flight Legs: Fill in the departure/arrival details for each part of your trip.

Generate Flight Table: Review your consolidated air travel summary.

Generate Smart Schedule: Launch the interactive schedule where your flights are pre-filled.

Polish & Export: Add your meeting notes, apply background colors, and export to Excel for offline use.

🌐 Technical Notes
Language: Interface is set to Traditional Chinese.

Privacy: This is a pure client-side tool. Your trip data never leaves your computer.

Requirement: Internet connection is required only for loading the ExcelJS library and Google Fonts.
